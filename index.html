Competition 03

League + Knockout

One season, two honors — same idea as the Indian Super League. The League phase crowns a Shield winner for topping the table. Then a knockout playoff, open to the league's top finishers, decides the actual season champion.

🛡️

The Shield and the Playoff Champion can be different players in the same season — the Shield rewards consistency across the league phase, the playoffs reward who peaks at the right time.
